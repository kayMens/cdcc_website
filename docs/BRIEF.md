# CDC Group Website Rebuild Plan

## 1. Project Goal

Rebuild the CDC Group website as a polished, corporate WordPress site inspired by the clarity, confidence, navigation, imagery, and case-study presentation of BCG.com while retaining a distinct CDC identity. The site must be easy for CDC staff to update without code, responsive on all common devices, and delivered within a controlled fixed-price scope.

## 2. Release 1 Scope

### Primary outcomes

- Communicate CDC's promise, three consulting capabilities, experience, people, partners, ventures, programmes, and contact details clearly.
- Present approved work through reusable project case studies and publish insights through reusable article templates.
- Provide straightforward browsing, search, sector/type filters, and working contact, Fellows, and Careers forms.
- Give CDC full administrator access, documented ownership, staff training, backups, and a defect-correction period after launch.

### Platform

- Price and build a clean, maintainable WordPress solution first.
- Audit the current WordPress installation before committing to reuse it; recommend reuse only if it can be securely updated and maintained.
- Any custom platform must be quoted separately, with the reason and ongoing costs clearly stated.

## 3. Information Architecture

Limit the primary navigation to these six sections:

1. **Capabilities**
   - Overview
   - Finance, Markets & Enterprise
   - Governance, Systems & Digital
   - Inclusive & Sustainable Development
2. **CDC Digital Hub**
   - SME Finance Ghana
   - Adansonia
   - DreamJobsGh
   - Provide short descriptions and external links only; do not build new venture software.
3. **Programmes & Platforms**
   - Agribusiness Academy
   - Creation Hub
   - SME Academy
   - Cross-link Adansonia if needed instead of duplicating content.
4. **Insights**
   - Articles
   - Voices from the Field
   - Throwback Series
   - Publications/PDF downloads
5. **CDC Fellows**
   - Programme information, eligibility, current intake status, application form, and FAQs.
6. **About CDC Group**
   - Story and identity
   - Leadership and team
   - CDC Consult Ltd, CDC & Associates, and Rufinlit LBG (legal name to be confirmed)
   - Active MOU partners

Make **Client Impact** a prominent header/homepage link. Keep **Careers**, **Contact**, and **Search** readily accessible outside or alongside the six main headings.

## 4. Homepage Requirements

Build the homepage in this order:

1. Header with CDC logo, primary navigation, Client Impact, Careers, Contact, and Search.
2. Hero with a strong CDC promise, approved professional image/visual, and two calls to action: **Our Capabilities** and **Contact Us**.
3. Capabilities section with three quality image cards for the consulting pillars.
4. Why CDC / selected experience section using substantiated examples rather than unapproved headline statistics.
5. Client Impact section featuring three or four approved projects and credible results or outputs.
6. Digital Hub strip featuring the three ventures and external links.
7. Insights section with three latest articles or field perspectives.
8. Approved partner logos, enquiry invitation, and footer contact/legal information.

Retain these eight sectors as filters: Agribusiness, Education, Financial Services, Health, Housing, Renewable Energy, Tourism, and WASH. Display project/partner totals only when explicitly approved; no statistics dashboard is required.

## 5. Reusable Content and Layouts

Design approximately 8–10 reusable layouts rather than a unique design for each entry. At minimum, provide templates for:

- Homepage
- Capability overview and capability pillar
- Digital Hub/venture and programme
- Client Impact listing and case study
- Insights listing and article
- About/member firm/team/partner
- CDC Fellows and Careers
- Contact, search, legal, and standard information pages

### Content models

- **Project case study:** title, client, sector, capability, year, featured image, summary, challenge, response, outcomes/outputs, approved claim, related content, and contact CTA.
- **Insight/article:** title, type, sector, author, date, image, body, downloadable PDF (optional), and related content.
- **Person:** name, role, headshot, biography, and optional contact/link details.
- **Partner/MOU organisation:** name, logo, brief description, status/approval, and link where relevant.
- **Venture/programme:** name, description, image/logo, external URL or detail page, and CTA.

Staff must be able to add and edit these items with the supplied layouts, without paying for new page design or writing code.

## 6. Initial Content Included in the Base Scope

- Homepage and all six primary navigation sections.
- Three capability pillar pages.
- Three member-firm profiles plus standard contact and legal pages.
- Up to 8 approved case studies and 8 insights/articles.
- Up to 12 team/leadership profiles.
- Up to 15 approved partner logos or brief partner profiles with active MOUs.
- Three Digital Hub ventures and up to three distinct programme pages.
- One CDC Fellows application form, a simple Careers/vacancy section, and one contact form.

The vendor must declare a fixed per-item upload price for content beyond these limits. CDC must approve all client logos, project claims, and published case-study material.

## 7. Functional Requirements

### Editing and administration

- Provide full WordPress administrator access to CDC.
- Enable non-technical staff to update copy, images, projects, articles, partner logos, and team profiles.
- Run two practical training sessions and supply a concise operating guide and all login details.

### Navigation, search, and filters

- Provide an expandable desktop navigation/mega menu and a simple, accessible mobile equivalent.
- Add site search.
- Add simple project and insight filters by relevant sector, capability, or content type.

### Forms

- Contact form sends enquiries to the CDC-designated mailbox.
- Fellows and Careers forms collect applications, allow private CV uploads, and indicate whether applications are open.
- Apply spam prevention, validation, secure upload handling, and tested email notifications.

### Technical quality

- Responsive across phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.
- HTTPS, protected administration, maintained updates, backups, and no visible production errors.
- Search-engine readiness: page titles, meta descriptions, clean URLs, XML sitemap/configuration as appropriate, and redirects from important legacy pages.
- Preserve CDC control of domains, hosting, administrator access, content, website files, and owned licences.

## 8. Explicit Exclusions

Do not include these unless separately approved and priced:

- Member/client accounts, saved articles, payments, complex applicant tracking, or custom databases.
- AI chatbot, interactive national map, advanced impact dashboard, or multilingual site.
- Paid CRM/newsletter tools, advertising, professional photography, or large-scale copywriting.
- Special animation or bespoke software intended to reproduce BCG's scale.

## 9. Delivery Phases and Approval Gates

### Phase 1: Confirm scope

Deliver final sitemap, reusable-layout inventory, content checklist/gap analysis, WordPress reuse assessment, fixed quotation, recurring costs, exclusions, and declared extra-content rates. CDC approves the included sections and work items.

### Phase 2: Visual approval

Before full build, present:

- Desktop and mobile homepage mock-ups.
- Open desktop navigation/mega-menu and mobile navigation.
- Capability page design.
- Case-study page design.

Include two reasonable design-revision rounds. CDC approval of the CDC-branded, BCG-inspired direction is required before implementation continues.

### Phase 3: Build and populate

Implement the approved reusable WordPress layouts, navigation, search, filters, forms, security configuration, redirects, and supplied initial content in a working preview environment. CDC reviews texts, media, case studies, external links, and usability.

### Phase 4: Test and prepare handover

Test mobile/desktop behaviour, browser basics, menus, internal/external links, search, filters, forms, email delivery, CV upload privacy, content editing, backups, redirects, and visible error handling. Demonstrate independent creation of a case study and article.

### Phase 5: Launch and handover

Launch only after content approval and agreed tests. Hand over full access, domains/hosting control, backups, redirects, training, credentials, operating guide, and ownership information. Provide at least 30 days of no-cost corrections for defects within the signed scope.

## 10. CDC Inputs and Decisions

CDC supplies approved copy, logos, photos, client/project information, names and biographies, programme descriptions, venture URLs, and current-site access. Before the launch date is agreed, identify gaps in those materials.

CDC must also confirm:

- Final wording of navigation labels.
- Legal name for Rufinlit LBG.
- Approved project outcomes/claims, client logos, partner logos, and active MOU partners.
- Whether project/partner totals may be published.
- Form recipient mailboxes, data-retention expectations, and whether Fellows/Careers applications are currently open.
- Important legacy URLs that require redirects.

## 11. Acceptance Checklist

- The homepage, desktop menu, and mobile menu visibly follow the approved CDC-branded, BCG-inspired designs.
- A first-time visitor can identify CDC, its three capabilities, and examples of client impact.
- All pages, navigation, links, search, filters, and forms work on desktop and mobile without visible technical errors.
- CDC staff can independently add a new article and case study using the reusable layouts.
- All included, approved content is uploaded before go-live.
- CDC controls the domain, hosting, content, administrator access, files, and licences it owns.

## 12. Commercial Guardrails

- Require a fixed total for the agreed base scope, plus taxes/VAT and separate annual/renewal costs.
- Separate one-time pricing for scope/design, build, forms, initial upload, transfer/launch, training/handover, and optional maintenance.
- Itemise hosting, domain, licences, and optional extras by provider and renewal date.
- Do not charge for reuse of an existing approved layout.
- Require written CDC approval before any scope variation starts; do not accept surprise invoices.
- Discuss the suggested payment schedule: 20% on approval, 30% after approved designs, 30% after working preview, and 20% after launch/handover.
- Score vendor proposals on appearance/clarity (30%), understanding of scope (25%), cost/recurring charges (25%), and support/track record (20%). Require two corporate website examples and a live editing-system demonstration.
