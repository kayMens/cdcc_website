How CDC helped turn sector insight into a practical programme for more resilient rural enterprises.

This prototype uses representative copy only. The live site would publish CDC-approved project context, claims, client references and outcomes, using the same structured format for every case study.

The challenge

Partners needed a clear view of the barriers affecting rural enterprises and a programme that could bring together market actors, service providers and decision-makers around priorities for action.

The approach

CDC combined stakeholder engagement, targeted analysis and collaborative programme design. The work focused on practical next steps, defined ownership and a way to track progress without creating unnecessary reporting burden.
